Non-Negotiable Guardrails

The platform governs itself.
No exceptions. No overrides.

These are not configuration options. They are platform-level commitments enforced at every layer — service, query, route, and AI boundary.

🔒
Tenant Isolation
Enforced in every service and query. Tenant context is always explicit — never inferred. No cross-tenant data leakage is architecturally possible.
👥
RBAC & Permissions
Admin · Supervisor · Employee · Project Supervisor (Responsibility). Role gates enforced before any action — no privilege escalation possible.
📋
Full Audit Trail
Every action is recorded with actor, timestamp, and context. No action occurs without a corresponding audit entry — governance is not optional.
📐
Approved Baseline
Immutable source before edits. No schedule performance, budget comparison, or earned-value calculation runs without an approved, locked baseline.
🔢
Data Integrity
No invented figures. No overrides. Budget, commitment, and schedule data comes from governed, approved records only — never inferred by AI.
🚫
No AI Overrides
AI cannot baseline, validate, approve, close, delete, or invent figures. Ask Me is advisory and read-only. Humans make every governed decision.
